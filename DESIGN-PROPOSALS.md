# Design Proposals

Larger or cross-cutting changes start as a short written proposal so the approach
can be discussed before code.

1. Open an issue titled `Proposal: <summary>`.
2. Describe the problem, the proposed approach, alternatives considered, and the
   blast radius (CRDs, runtime, dashboard, crews).
3. A maintainer labels it `proposal` and the discussion happens in the issue.
4. Accepted proposals are implemented; significant ones also land an ADR under
   `docs/adr/`.

_A formal enhancement-proposal (KEP-style) process may be adopted later._
